How the likelihood % is calculated

Each rumour gets a blended score from four weighted signals. There's no single "transfer probability" feed, so this combines the things that actually predict whether a deal happens:

Deal stage (40%)How far the deal has progressed — from "monitoring / admirer" up to "agreed, here we go". The single biggest predictor.
Source reliability (30%)Who's reporting it. Tier-1 (Fabrizio Romano, Sky Sports, club-confirmed) scores high; single tabloid or foreign-paper speculation scores low.
Consensus / volume (20%)How many credible outlets corroborate it, and whether the story is building or going quiet.
Market signal (10%)Bookmaker "next club" odds where a market exists, plus fee-gap realism (how far apart the clubs are on price).

Scores are a judgement-based estimate, not a guarantee — treat them as a ranking guide, not betting advice.
